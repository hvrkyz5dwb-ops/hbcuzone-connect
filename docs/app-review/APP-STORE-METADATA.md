# PlugU — App Store Metadata and Screenshot Plan

## Short description

PlugU is a students-only campus commerce and services app built for HBCU
students and student-owned businesses. Buy, sell, book and build on your
campus.

## Long description (draft)

PlugU connects verified students at the same school. Book a barber, stylist,
nail tech, photographer, tutor, cook or driver who studies where you study.
Sell clothes, dorm goods and tickets. Find campus events, student orgs,
scholarships and internships. Everything is scoped to your verified campus,
and you can explore other PlugU campuses from the same app.

Membership requires a school-issued `.edu` address, or a manual review for
schools that do not issue one. Every member can report or block anyone, and
reported content disappears immediately.

## How PlugU differs from a generic marketplace

| Generic marketplace | PlugU |
| --- | --- |
| Open sign-up | School-email verification with Unverified / Pending / Verified states |
| Radius-based location | Campus-scoped feeds, zones, places and campus-only map |
| Goods listings | Goods **and** bookable student services with availability slots |
| Seller ratings only | Verified Student badges, earned badges, campus rankings |
| Generic feed | Campus sections: Happening at [School], Services Near You, Campus Events, Trending Student Businesses, Scholarships and Opportunities |
| Separate regional apps | One Bundle ID, in-app campus switcher |

## Strongest screens for screenshots

1. **Home** — campus bar with school name and Verified Student pill, HBCU
   purpose headline, Happening at [School].
2. **Market** — campus-scoped listings with category chips and the filter panel
   open on **Verified students only**.
3. **Student service profile** — a seller profile with Verified Student badge,
   rating, services and booking action.
4. **Campus event** — event detail with RSVP.
5. **Search** — campus filters and mixed results (listings, services, events).
6. **Safety** — the three-dot content menu with Report / Hide / Block, or the
   report reason sheet.

Only ship screenshots of screens that exist today with real app content. Do not
advertise unreleased features.
