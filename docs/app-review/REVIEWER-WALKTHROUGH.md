## Step-by-step

1. **Launch.** The intro plays and is skippable with **Skip**.
2. **Sign in.** On the account screen tap **Sign in**, enter the credentials
   above, tap **Sign in**. No verification step appears.
3. **Verification state.** At the top of Home, the campus bar shows the school
   name with a green **Verified Student** pill.
4. **Campus feed.** Scroll Home: Happening at [School], Student Services Near
   You, Buy and Sell on Campus, Campus Events, Trending Student Businesses,
   Scholarships and Opportunities, and Campus Safety and Community Standards.
5. **Switch campus.** Tap **Switch campus** in the campus bar, pick another
   HBCU, confirm the feed re-scopes, then tap **Back to [home campus]**.
6. **Marketplace + filters.** Open **Market**. Use the category chips, then the
   filter button: School, Sellers (**Verified students only**), Sort, Max
   price, Fulfillment.
7. **Search.** Open **Search**, query a service such as "braids" or "tutoring";
   results are campus-scoped first.
8. **Seller profile.** Open any listing, then tap the seller to view their
   profile, Verified Student badge, rating and other listings.
9. **Campus event.** From Home → Campus Events (or the Events screen), open an
   event and view details and RSVP.
10. **Message a seller.** From a listing, tap **Message** to open an
    order-related conversation and send a message.
11. **Report a listing.** On any listing tap the three-dot menu → **Report
    listing**, choose a reason, add details, submit. The listing disappears
    from your feed immediately and the report is stored server-side.
12. **Block and unblock.** Three-dot menu → **Block user**, confirm. Their
    content disappears. Go to **Settings → Blocked users**, unblock with the
    confirmation prompt.
13. **Location prompt.** Open **Map**. The permission screen explains why
    location is used and offers **Continue** and **Not Now**. Choosing Not Now
    is never re-prompted and the rest of the app stays fully usable; the map is
    campus-only by design.
14. **Policies and support.** Settings → Help & Safety for Terms, Privacy,
    Community Standards, Safety Center and Contact support.
15. **Account deletion.** Settings → Delete account. The flow is real and
    permanent — do not run it on the demo account.
